    Known limits

    • Damage variance, criticals, and action choice are stochastic; action order follows Agility with no random roll.
    • Commands are committed at the start of each turn; follow-ups are chosen when the Attack lands.
    • Guard, emotions, Aubrey/Hero follow-ups and Omori’s “did not succumb” are not modeled yet.
    • Space Ex-Boyfriend and Space Bunny move choice is still provisional.
    • Levels 1–50 use exact class stat tables from the source data.
    • The standard inventory is an explicit scenario preset, not a claim about every save file.
    • “Best found” is a strong heuristic policy, not a proof of optimal play.
    • Custom calibration is local and descriptive; image extraction does not call a model yet.
